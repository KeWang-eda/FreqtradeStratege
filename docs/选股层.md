# Selection layer

The selection layer compares candidates at the same timestamp. It does not read other timestamps, set leverage, or place orders.

## Current implementation

`strategies/selection_layer.py` provides `rank_cross_sectional_candidates()`:

```text
net_edge = expected_edge - risk_penalty × predicted_adverse_risk - execution_cost
```

Long and short candidates are ranked independently. A candidate is selected only when it is within `Top-K` and its net edge meets the minimum threshold.

## Real-data smoke

The independent smoke used 54 local 1h futures files and 574 timestamps:

- Candidate rows: 14,634.
- Top-K: 3 per side.
- Selected rows: 2,490.
- Direction-aware Precision@K: 52.97%.
- Selected direction-aware future 8-bar return: 0.4608% mean.
- All-candidate direction-aware future 8-bar return: 0.3952% mean.
- Pair-set turnover proxy: 12.84% mean.

These are signal-selection diagnostics, not a fee-complete strategy backtest. Portfolio, execution, and validation layers must still run before any promotion decision.

## Relative-strength mechanism audit

A pre-registered control ranked 15 Binance perpetuals by past 4-hour, 12-hour, or 24-hour return, held the top three long and bottom three short for 8 hours, and charged fixed 2x leverage, local funding, fees, and slippage. All three lookbacks failed after cost:

- 4-hour: gross mean `-0.0451%`, net mean `-0.3283%`, positive fraction `48.57%`.
- 12-hour: gross mean `-0.0050%`, net mean `-0.2893%`, positive fraction `48.87%`.
- 24-hour: gross mean `-0.0014%`, net mean `-0.2852%`, positive fraction `49.18%`.

The simple cross-sectional mechanism is not connected to the strategy. Further changes to the existing momentum family require a new information source or a new market mechanism, not another lookback sweep.

## Funding carry mechanism audit

A separate funding-only control used the last known funding rate at each 8-hour decision: positive funding selected shorts, negative funding selected longs, with three positions per side and real funding, fee, and slippage accounting. Across 1,122 decision periods and 6,166 position events, the wallet returned `-10.76%`, with Sharpe `-3.12` and maximum relative drawdown `11.64%`. Funding carry is not connected to the strategy.
